# Westpac (Australia) – customer-facing policies: research notes

Researched: 2026-10-28. Sources: westpac.com.au pages and westpac.com.au PDFs only.

Notes on sourcing:
- PDF quotes were taken from text extracted directly from the downloaded PDF, so they are verbatim.
- HTML page quotes came through a fetch tool that summarises pages. Only short excerpts that the tool returned as quoted text are used here, but spot-check them against the live page before publishing.
- Most HTML pages show no "last updated" date. Where a PDF has an effective date or a print code (e.g. `WBCCCB003L 0921` means Sept 2021), it is given.
- Where sources conflict (for example phone hours), both are recorded and the conflict is flagged.

## 3. Accessibility, vulnerable customers and extra care

### Extra care (general)
- **Fact:** Westpac says the ABA Banking Code of Practice guides its approach to extra care for customers in vulnerable circumstances. Circumstances covered include elder financial abuse, domestic violence and financial abuse, gambling, dementia, disasters and life events such as bereavement or separation.
  - **Quote:** "Westpac's approach to providing extra care for customers in vulnerable circumstances" (is guided by the Banking Code of Practice)
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/sustainability/our-positions-and-perspectives/difficult-circumstances/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

  - **Quote:** "Westpac customers who need support can contact Westpac on 1800 063 509"

### Deceased estates (bereavement)
- **Fact:** Anyone can tell Westpac that a customer has died: by phone, at a branch (appointment recommended), by email, or through deathnotification.gov.au if the death was registered in Australia.
  - **Quote:** "Anyone can let us know when someone's died."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/help/lifemoments/managing-unplanned-moments/deceased-estates/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Westpac Estates Management team: 1300 130 240 (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm AEST), +61 2 9155 7590 from overseas, estatesmanagement@westpac.com.au. Deceased home loan queries go to 132 558.
  - **Quote:** "You can call us on 1300 130 240, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (AEST) or send an email to estatesmanagement@westpac.com.au." … "If you have questions about a deceased person's home loan, please call 132 558."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/Solutions/wbc-support-guide.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: April 2026 (WBC01616 0426)

- **Fact:** To notify, provide a completed Deceased Customer Notification & Estate Form plus one certified proof of death (death certificate, medical or police report, or probate / letters of administration). Send certified copies, not originals. The documents can be emailed, or taken to a branch by appointment.
  - **Quote:** "A completed Deceased Customer Notification & Estate Form, and • One document that confirms your loved one has passed away"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/Solutions/wbc-support-guide.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: April 2026

- **Fact:** Within 14 business days of notification, Westpac writes to confirm what it has done and the next steps. Once it has all the documents, it finalises estate instructions within 14 business days.
  - **Quote:** "Within 14 business days, we'll send you correspondence confirming what we've done and outline the next steps." … "The process will be completed within 14 business days"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/Solutions/wbc-support-guide.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: April 2026

- **Fact:** Only the next of kin, the executor or a legal representative of the estate can manage the estate. Account information is given only to an Authorised Estate Representative.
  - **Quote:** "Only the Next of Kin or Executor can manage the estate, or a legal representative of the estate."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/help/lifemoments/managing-unplanned-moments/deceased-estates/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** After notification, sole accounts are restrained, which stops direct debits and recurring payments, though deposits can still arrive. Loan repayments must continue and interest still accrues. Joint account holders keep access, and accounts pass to the surviving holder once Westpac receives the death certificate.
  - **Quote:** "Loan repayments must continue, interest will accrue" … "Joint account holders keep access as usual"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/help/lifemoments/managing-unplanned-moments/deceased-estates/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Funeral costs and some other estate expenses (ATO bills, rates, the deceased's utilities and medical bills, probate application fees) can be paid directly from the estate once Westpac has the invoice and forms. Credit cards held solely in the deceased's name are blocked, including for additional cardholders.
  - **Quote:** "Providing the funeral invoice allows us to pay the funeral director directly from your loved one's estate"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/Solutions/wbc-support-guide.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: April 2026

- **Fact:** Simpler documents are needed if the estate balance is under $100,000 with no secured lending. Grant of Probate or Letters of Administration is required if the balance is $100,000 or more, the estate includes secured lending, or an "Estate of the Late" account is wanted. That account can only be set up at a branch.
  - **Quote:** "If the Estate balance is equal to, or greater than $100,000 or contains secured lending products or you wish to create an Estate of the Late account"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/Solutions/wbc-support-guide.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: April 2026

- **Fact:** Westpac recognises three arrangements. A power of attorney (general or enduring) is set up in a branch, with legal paperwork and an appointment. An account signatory operates the account in a branch. An authorised third party (e.g. a financial counsellor) can speak with Westpac by phone but cannot access the accounts.
  - **Quote:** "In-branch process (legal paperwork and appointment required)" … "They will not have direct access to your accounts."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/help/support-with-tough-times/getting-help-from-someone-you-trust/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** An enduring power of attorney must be set up while the person still has capacity to make decisions.
  - **Quote:** "You must set up an enduring power of attorney while you still have capacity to make decisions."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/help/support-with-tough-times/getting-help-from-someone-you-trust/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

## 5. Inactive / unclaimed accounts and account closure

- **Fact:** A personal deposit account becomes inactive if there are no deposits or withdrawals for a continuous six months and the balance is nil, or in debit without an arrangement. Westpac may close an inactive account without notice.
  - **Quote:** "If your account becomes inactive we may close your account without any notice to you."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/PersonalAccounts-tandc.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: 27 August 2026

- **Fact:** Money is "unclaimed" when an account holds at least $500 and has had no customer deposits or withdrawals for 7 years or longer. Interest and fees do not count as activity. Westpac must close these accounts and send the funds to ASIC by 31 March each year.
  - **Quote:** "Funds in bank accounts are considered "unclaimed" when the account has a balance ≥ $500" … "Interest earned and fees charged do NOT constitute items that can activate an account."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/personal-banking/bank-accounts/manage/unclaimed-moneys-changes-legislation/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Westpac will usually notify customers before closing an account under unclaimed money rules, for example by letter, email, message centre or phone. It may be unable to if its address records are out of date.
  - **Quote:** "We will usually notify you before we close your account, but there may be circumstances where we are not able to (such as where our record of your address is out of date)."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/PersonalAccounts-tandc.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: 27 August 2026

- **Fact:** To reclaim money, the customer searches ASIC's Moneysmart, notes the Original Transaction Number, and takes the completed form to a Westpac branch. They need two forms of ID (one with a photo) and proof of account ownership. The process can take up to 3 months. The Bump Savings account is exempt until the holder turns 18.
  - **Quote:** "can take up to 3 months"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/personal-banking/bank-accounts/manage/unclaimed-moneys-changes-legislation/ (Bump exemption: PersonalAccounts-tandc.pdf, effective 27 Aug 2026)
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Customers can close eligible everyday and savings accounts, credit cards and Flexi Loan accounts in Online Banking or the Westpac App. They can also visit a branch or call 132 032 (8am–8pm Sydney time, 7 days; +61 2 92939270 from overseas).
  - **Quote:** "8am-8pm Sydney time, 7 days a week"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/faq/close-account/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** When closing an account, the customer must return any cards, unused cheques and passbooks. Cheque accounts close only after all outstanding cheques are presented. Customers must update anyone who pays into or debits the account and cancel periodical payments and direct debits themselves.
  - **Quote:** "It is your responsibility to notify anyone who either directly credits or debits your accounts (including ATO, Medicare and accountant/tax agent)."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/PersonalAccounts-tandc.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective date: 27 August 2026

- **Fact:** Westpac may close an account after reasonable notice, for example for unsatisfactory conduct, business use of a personal account, or use that harms another person such as financial abuse. In some cases it may act without prior notice, but says it will act fairly and reasonably.

## 6. Financial Claims Scheme (FCS)

- **Fact:** The FCS is an Australian Government scheme. If the government activates it after a bank fails, it protects certain deposits up to $250,000 per account holder per eligible ADI. APRA administers it and aims to pay most customers within seven calendar days.
  - **Quote:** "protection and quick access to deposits" … "within seven calendar days"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/faq/financial-claims-scheme/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** The $250,000 limit applies to a customer's combined deposits under one banking licence. For Westpac that includes Westpac, St.George, Bank of Melbourne and BankSA, plus eligible Westpac products sold under third-party brands.
  - **Quote:** (FAQ lists Westpac, Bank of Melbourne, St.George and BankSA under the single licence limit)
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/faq/financial-claims-scheme/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Westpac's deposit T&Cs say deposit account holders may be entitled to an FCS payment in certain circumstances, that payments are limited per customer, and that more information is at www.fcs.gov.au.
  - **Quote:** "If you have a deposit account with us, you may be entitled to a payment under the Financial Claims Scheme (FCS) in certain circumstances." … "Payments under the Financial Claims Scheme are limited for each customer."
