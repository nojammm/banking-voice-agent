## 2. Security features and what Westpac will never ask

- **Fact:** SafeBlock lets customers lock their accounts and cards instantly in the App or Online Banking (search "SafeBlock"). It is meant for when they suspect fraud or think they've shared details with a scammer.
  - **Quote:** "SafeBlock is a security feature you can use if you suspect fraud or think you've shared details with a scammer." / "Instantly lock your accounts and cards if something doesn't feel right – then unlock when you're ready."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-safeblock/ ; https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** SafeBlock blocks new Online Banking payments and transfers, new card purchases and ATM withdrawals, and most recurring card payments. It does not stop deposits, direct debits, scheduled payments set up with an account number (not a card), branch transactions or telephone banking. Unlocking may need ID verification and can take time. It needs App version 11.8.0 or later.
  - **Quote:** "Unlocking your account can take time."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-safeblock/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** SafeCall is an in-app calling feature. Optus verifies that the call is really from Westpac. Not every genuine Westpac call is a SafeCall, and only Australian-registered mobile numbers are eligible.
  - **Quote:** "SafeCall is an Australian-first in-app calling feature designed to help you know when it's really us calling." / "Optus will verify the call is coming from us, so you know it's legitimate." / "not all calls will be a SafeCall."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-safecall/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** SaferPay asks extra questions when a payment shows signs of a scam. If the answers raise concerns, Westpac will stop the payment. A payment classed as consistent with scam activity won't be processed.
  - **Quote:** "If your answers raise concerns, we'll prevent the money from leaving your account." / "If we've categorised your payment as consistent with scam activity, the payment won't be processed."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-saferpay/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** When you pay someone new by BSB and account number, Westpac Verify with Confirmation of Payee checks the details. It compares them with the receiving bank's records and with Westpac's own payment data. Results include "Everything checks out", "Details don't match", "Worth a second look" and "Try again later". The check is only available in Online Banking and the App.
  - **Quote:** "The details you've entered don't match the account details held by the receiving financial institution." / "is only available in Westpac Online Banking and Mobile App."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-verify/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** If Verify shows a mismatch, Westpac advises calling the payee to confirm their details. It warns that money sent to the wrong account may not be recoverable.
  - **Quote:** "Call the payee to confirm the details or check you've not entered account details incorrectly." / "If you send money to the wrong account, we may not be able to recover your funds."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-verify/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Eligible individual and sole trader customers can ask to opt out of Confirmation of Payee. If they opt out, payers won't see their account name.
  - **Quote:** "eligible customers (i.e. individual and sole trader customers) can request to opt out of Confirmation of Payee"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-verify/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Payments to a first-time payee may take longer, as may payments held for extra security screening. Paying someone for the first time usually requires a Security Code. The page doesn't state how long the delay is.
  - **Quote:** "Payments may take longer in some instances ... there is a payment to a first time payee or when the payment is caught for additional security screening" / "When paying someone for the first time you will likely be asked to enter a Security Code."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/articles/creating-a-payee/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Westpac says Online Banking is protected by 24/7 fraud monitoring.
  - **Quote:** "Your Online Banking is protected by 24/7 fraud monitoring, specially trained teams, and security features"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Westpac will never ask you to share Online Banking Security Codes or passwords, or your credit card expiry date, PIN or CVC. It will never ask you to move money to a "safe" account or another bank, or to help catch cyber criminals or assist with internal investigations.
  - **Quote:** "Share your Online Banking Security Codes or passwords" / "Share your Credit Card expiry date, PIN or CVC" / "Move money to a "safe" account, or to an account with another bank" / "Help us catch cyber criminals or assist with internal investigations"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Westpac will never send links asking for personal or financial information. It will never ask you to share your password or Security Code, ask you to install software that connects to your device (remote access), or send a link that opens its sign-in page directly.
  - **Quote:** "Westpac will never send you any links requesting your personal or financial information or ask you to share your password or Security Code, install software to connect to your device or send you a link that directly opens our sign-in page."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/security/wbc_security-scam_6pp_flyer_online_banking.pdf (same text in the card flyer)
  - **Page date / version:** "WL1766 05/25"

- **Fact:** Westpac will never ask you to reply to an email to confirm or update your details. It will never send an email or SMS with a link that goes directly to its sign-in page.
  - **Quote:** "We will never ask you to reply to an email to confirm or update your details." / "an email or SMS with a link that directly goes to our sign in page."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/how-we-protect-you/westpac-id-secure/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

## 3. Unauthorised transactions and refund guarantees

- **Fact:** Under the Online Banking Security Guarantee, Westpac will repay missing funds if a Westpac account is compromised through internet fraud. The customer must comply with the Online Banking Terms and Conditions, which includes keeping sign-in details private. The page says the guarantee does not apply to Corporate Online Banking.
  - **Quote:** "If your Westpac account is compromised as a result of Internet fraud, we guarantee to repay any missing funds, providing you comply with our Online Banking Terms and Conditions. This includes keeping your sign-in details (including passwords, Westpac Protect™ SMS codes and SecurID® Token codes) private."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/ ; https://www.westpac.com.au/protect
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** The Online Banking Security Guarantee also requires the customer to tell Westpac immediately if they suspect their access details are compromised, or suspect an unauthorised transaction or fraud.
  - **Quote:** "You must inform us immediately if you suspect the security of your access details has been compromised, or you suspect an unauthorised transaction or potential fraud on your accounts."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/security/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Under the Westpac Fraud Money Back Guarantee, customers are reimbursed for unauthorised card transactions, provided they did not contribute to the loss and contacted Westpac promptly. The card's conditions of use set out the full details, including when the customer is liable.
  - **Quote:** "Our Westpac Fraud Money Back Guarantee ensures that you will be reimbursed for any unauthorised card transactions provided that you have not contributed to the loss and contacted Westpac promptly. Refer to your card's conditions of use for full details, including when you will be liable."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/security/wbc_security-scam_6pp_flyer_card.pdf ; https://www.westpac.com.au/protect
  - **Page date / version:** "WL1767 05/25"

- **Fact:** Card fraud investigations normally take 21 days, and complex cases can take up to 45 days. For eligible customers, money is refunded to the card first, and Westpac then tries to recover it through chargeback.
  - **Quote:** "Normal cases take 21 days. More complex cases can take up to 45 days." / "If eligible the money gets refunded to your card, so you are not out of pocket. The bank then attempts to recover funds through the chargeback process."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/security/wbc_security-scam_6pp_flyer_card.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "WL1767 05/25"

- **Fact:** Standard online banking fraud investigations take up to 21 days, and complex cases may take longer. If eligible under the Online Banking Security Guarantee, funds are credited back to the account they were debited from. Customers keep the same customer number but get a new password.
  - **Quote:** "For standard cases the investigation will take up to 21 days." / "If you're eligible for a refund, we'll notify you and credit the funds back to the account where your transactions were debited." / "No, your customer number will remain the same."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/security/wbc_security-scam_6pp_flyer_online_banking.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "WL1766 05/25"

- **Fact:** Under the personal Debit Mastercard terms, the account holder is not liable for unauthorised transactions where it is clear the user did not contribute to the loss. This includes transactions made after Westpac was notified of a lost, stolen or misused card, and duplicate debits.
  - **Quote:** "The account holder will not be liable for losses resulting from unauthorised transactions where it is clear that the user has not contributed to the loss."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/docs/pdf/pb/Debit_MasterCard_TandC.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "Effective date: 27 August 2026"

- **Fact:** The account holder is liable for unauthorised transactions caused by the user doing any of the following: engaging in fraud, voluntarily disclosing codes (including to family or friends), writing the PIN on the card, or acting with extreme carelessness. The user is also liable for losses caused by unreasonable delay in reporting.
  - **Quote:** "voluntarily disclosing any of their codes to anyone, including a family member or friend" / "writing their PIN on their card" / "acting with extreme carelessness in failing to protect their codes."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/docs/pdf/pb/Debit_MasterCard_TandC.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "Effective date: 27 August 2026"

- **Fact:** If a code was required and it's unclear whether the user contributed to the loss, the account holder's liability is capped at the least of three amounts: $150, the account balance (including pre-arranged credit), or the actual loss at the time Westpac is notified. Liability is determined under the ePayments Code where that Code applies.
  - **Quote:** "The account holder's liability in such cases will be the least of: • $150; or • the balance of the account(s), including any pre-arranged credit; or • the actual loss at the time we are notified"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/docs/pdf/pb/Debit_MasterCard_TandC.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "Effective date: 27 August 2026"

- **Fact:** An older Westpac credit card page says Westpac sends a "Transaction Investigation Request" form, which the customer must sign and return within 14 days of receiving it. Cases can take up to 21 working days. This page may be outdated (see note).
  - **Quote:** (fetch tool summary) the customer must "return within 14 days of receiving it"; "Our Westpac Fraud Money Back Guarantee ensures that customers will be reimbursed for any unauthorised transactions"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/credit-cards/manage/fraud-disputes-lost-stolen-cards/fraudulent-transaction
  - **Page date / version:** not shown. Note: a search result flagged this page as several years old, so it is lower confidence than the 2025 flyers.

## 4. Disputing a card transaction / chargebacks

- **Fact:** Westpac suggests contacting the merchant first, because most issues are resolved faster that way.
  - **Quote:** "Most issues can be resolved more quickly by contacting the merchant directly."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/personal-banking/credit-cards/manage/fraud-disputes-lost-stolen-cards/disputing-transaction/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** To lodge a card dispute in the App or Online Banking, select the transaction and choose "Report this transaction". To lodge by phone, call 132 032. Supporting documents can be uploaded.
  - **Quote:** "Select the transaction, then select 'Report this transaction'" / "If you need assistance, contact us on 132 032 and ask to lodge a card dispute." / "You can upload supporting documents when submitting your dispute."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/personal-banking/credit-cards/manage/fraud-disputes-lost-stolen-cards/disputing-transaction/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Pending transactions can't be disputed until they have processed, which usually takes up to 5 business days.
  - **Quote:** "Pending transactions can't be disputed until they have processed." / "This usually takes up to 5 business days, but in some cases may take longer."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/personal-banking/credit-cards/manage/fraud-disputes-lost-stolen-cards/disputing-transaction/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Disputes are usually resolved within 45 days of Westpac receiving the information it needs. Westpac may apply a temporary credit while it reviews a dispute.
  - **Quote:** "Disputes are usually resolved within 45 days of receiving the information required to investigate the claim." / "In some circumstances, we may apply a temporary credit to your account while the dispute is being reviewed."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/personal-banking/credit-cards/manage/fraud-disputes-lost-stolen-cards/disputing-transaction/
  - **Page date / version:** not shown

- **Fact:** Debit Mastercard disputes must follow card scheme timeframes. For Mastercard, that is within 120 days of the transaction. Customers should contact Westpac as soon as possible, and late notice may prevent an investigation. These time limits may not apply where the ePayments Code applies.
  - **Quote:** "We must comply with card scheme rules which set out dispute procedures and notification timeframes (Mastercard: within 120 days of the transaction). If you don't notify us promptly, we may not be able to investigate your dispute within the time limits of card scheme rules. The timeframe for disputing a transaction may not apply where the ePayments Code applies."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/docs/pdf/pb/Debit_MasterCard_TandC.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "Effective date: 27 August 2026"

- **Fact:** A chargeback is when Westpac charges a disputed transaction back to the merchant under card scheme rules. It isn't available for all transactions. For example, BPAY payments can't be charged back. Westpac claims a chargeback if the customer asks within the scheme time limit and provides the supporting information it reasonably needs.
  - **Quote:** "This is known as a chargeback." / "Chargeback rights are not available for all types of transactions. For example, we cannot claim a chargeback in relation to BPAY Payments from the account." / "you give us the information and material we reasonably require to support a chargeback, Otherwise any chargeback right we have may be lost."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/docs/pdf/pb/Debit_MasterCard_TandC.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "Effective date: 27 August 2026"

- **Fact:** Complaints line under the Debit Mastercard terms: 1300 130 467, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week from anywhere in Australia. Westpac aims to resolve complaints at the first point of contact ("Ask Once" promise).
  - **Quote:** "You can contact us 24 hours a day, 7 days a week from anywhere in Australia, by: Telephone: 1300 130 467" / "This is our 'Ask Once' promise."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/docs/pdf/pb/Debit_MasterCard_TandC.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** "Effective date: 27 August 2026"

## 5. Privacy Statement and Credit Reporting Policy

- **Fact:** The current Westpac Privacy Statement says it is effective from August 2026 and was published in April 2026. It includes the Privacy Collection Notice, the Statement of Notifiable Matters, the Privacy Policy and the Credit Reporting Policy.
  - **Quote:** "Effective date: August 2026" / "This Privacy Statement was published in April 2026." / "This Privacy Statement includes our: Privacy Collection Notice ... Statement of Notifiable Matters ... Privacy Policy ... and Credit Reporting Policy"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf (HTML version: https://www.westpac.com.au/privacy/privacy-statement/)
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026; code "WBCPBB001 0826"

- **Fact:** Westpac collects personal and contact details, government ID (e.g. passport, driver's licence, Medicare number), call and security camera recordings, transaction records, and website and app behaviour data. It also collects device data such as device IDs and IP addresses.
  - **Quote:** "Your Medicare number, passport details, driver's licence number, director identification number, healthcare identifiers and copies of government identification documents." / "Call recordings when you contact our call centres or branches"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026

- **Fact:** The sensitive information Westpac collects includes citizenship, residency status and biometric data, used to verify identity and authorise transactions. In limited circumstances, it may also collect health or ethnic origin information.
  - **Quote:** "Information relating to your citizenship, residency status and biometric data (to verify your identity and authorise transactions"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026

- **Fact:** Westpac collects personal information to provide products and services, to verify identity and prevent fraud and crime, and to comply with legal and regulatory requirements in Australia and overseas.
  - **Quote:** "verify your identity" / "prevent, detect, report, investigate, disrupt and respond to suspicious, fraudulent, criminal or other activity" / "comply with our legislative and regulatory requirements (both in Australia and overseas)"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026

- **Fact:** Customers don't have to give Westpac their personal information. If they don't, Westpac may be unable to process applications, help with enquiries or respond to complaints.
  - **Quote:** "If you choose not to, we may not be able to process your application for a product or service, assist with your enquiries, provide all of the features available for a product or service, or respond to any complaint to us."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026

- **Fact:** Westpac may share information overseas. Recipients include service providers likely located in New Zealand, the US, Canada, India, Japan, the Philippines, the UK, Malaysia, Brazil, South Africa and the European Economic Area. They also include Westpac Group companies in Germany, Hong Kong, Singapore, New Zealand, the UK, the US, Fiji and Papua New Guinea.
  - **Quote:** "Westpac Group companies located in Germany, Hong Kong, Singapore, New Zealand, UK, United States, Fiji and Papua New Guinea"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026

- **Fact:** Customers can request access to, or correction of, their personal information by contacting Westpac. Westpac verifies identity first. Corrections are free. Westpac may charge a reasonable fee for providing access. If it refuses access or correction, it gives written reasons and explains how to complain.
  - **Quote:** "There is no fee for requesting corrections to your personal information or for us to make those corrections. In some circumstances, we may charge you a reasonable amount for providing you access to your personal information" / "We will seek to verify your identity before we allow access, or make changes"
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026

- **Fact:** For privacy complaints, customers should contact Westpac first. Westpac aims to resolve complaints within 5 business days. Complaints not resolved at first contact go to the Customer Solutions team.
  - **Quote:** "Our aim is to resolve your complaint within five (5) business days, and where possible we will resolve your complaint on the spot." / "we will then refer the complaint to our dedicated Customer Managers in our Customer Solutions team."
  - **URL:** https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/privacy/wbc-privacy-statement.pdf
  - **Page date / version:** Effective August 2026
