Westpac customers can make a complaint using Westpac's secure online complaints form, by signing in to Westpac Online Banking or the Westpac App, by calling 132 032 (8am to 8pm, 7 days, except public holidays), by visiting a Westpac branch, or by post to Westpac Customer Solutions, Reply Paid 5265, Sydney NSW 2001. Complaints about Auto Finance (now handled by Resimac on 1300 904 639), life insurance (TAL on 1300 209 088) and general insurance (Allianz on 1300 650 255) go to those providers.

Westpac's Personal Accounts Terms and Conditions (effective 27 August 2026) say Westpac aims to resolve a complaint within 5 business days, and on the spot where possible. If Westpac needs more time it will let the customer know. Complaints not resolved at first contact are referred to Westpac's Customer Solutions team. A customer who is not satisfied with Westpac's response or handling of a complaint can go to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA).

Westpac's Personal Accounts Terms and Conditions (effective 27 August 2026) say that if a customer is not satisfied with Westpac's response or handling of a complaint, they can contact the external dispute resolution scheme, the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA). AFCA is free and independent. AFCA contact details: online www.afca.org.au, email info@afca.org.au, phone 1800 931 678 (free call), post GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001.

## Westpac fairgo complaint principles

Westpac reviews complaints under six "fairgo" principles: Fair, Action, Integrity, Respect, Genuine and Open. Under the Action principle Westpac says it will respond in a timely way to resolve complaints and assess remediation as quickly as possible. Westpac also says it will acknowledge and apologise to customers when it has got things wrong.

## Westpac Customer Advocate: what the role does

The Westpac Group Customer Advocate's primary role is to advise and guide Westpac's complaint team, and the Advocate is independent of Westpac's business units. Westpac's Customer Advocate page says the Customer Advocate does not review individual complaints. Customers with a complaint should raise it with Westpac branch or phone staff, their relationship manager, or through Westpac's online Secure Feedback Form. The Customer Advocate is not a channel for reviewing the outcome of an individual complaint.
