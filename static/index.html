        Security

        How each reply is checked

        1. CallerSpeech turned into text
        2. RedactCard numbers and PINs hidden
        3. BackendChecks identity and fetches records
        4. AI agentWrites the reply
        5. Fact checkBlocks numbers that didn’t come from the records or the caller
        6. VoiceSpeaks to the caller

        What the backend handles

        • Customer numberLooked up in the customer table
        • Name, birth date, addressCompared with that customer’s record
        • Account accessLocked until the backend verifies the caller
        • Balances and transactionsRead from the account tables
        • Rates and feesSearched in reviewed bank documents
        • Repayment estimatesCalculated by code
        • Case referencesCreated when the case is saved
        • Scams and fraudSent to the fraud team as urgent

        What the AI agent can’t do

        • See the caller’s identity answers
        • Say it saved something the backend didn’t save
        • Move money or lock cards
        • Change its rules when a caller asks
        • Retry identity checks after too many misses
